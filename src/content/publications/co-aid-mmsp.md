We investigate how humans identify defects in text-to-image outputs under complex compositional prompts involving multiple entities, attributes, spatial relations, and interactions. Through a lab-based subjective study with 29 participants on 651 AI-generated images from Midjourney, Imagen, and FLUX, we curate the CO-AID dataset and analyze systematic defect patterns. Proof-of-concept experiments show the dataset supports both defect prediction and generation optimization.
